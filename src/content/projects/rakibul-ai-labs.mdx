Welcome to my lab. This is where I try things, break them, and write down what I learn along the way.

Every experiment here is a real build: what I tried, what failed, and what finally worked. Nothing is polished for show, because the mistakes are usually the most useful part.

## What You'll Find Here

- **Local AI projects:** models and agents that run fully on my own machine.
- **Build notes:** the setup, the tools, and the decisions behind each experiment.
- **Lessons learned:** the bugs, dead ends, and fixes from trial and error.
